<div class="novel-definition">
  <strong>Asiyah</strong> (<em>ah-see-YAH</em>): Noun. Hebrew.
  <em>The physical world we currently live in. A world of action.
  The fourth and lowest realm of existence. A place where the
  Creator hides from its creation.</em>

unassuming man in the Midwest. A small team is assembled to

far beyond imagining.
